# Do we take this insurance?

## Where the answer lives

1. **`V:\Faxes\2026 APC Not Contracted Plans.xlsx`.** This is the current source (last updated 7/7/2026). It has two tabs:
   - **APC CONTRACTED PLANS:** networks and plans we're in, such as First Health Network, Multiplan, PHCS, Beech Street, Galaxy Health Network, HealthSmart, CIGNA PPO, Three Rivers, and some Medicare Advantage plans.
   - **Not Contracted Plans:** specific HMOs, medical groups and narrow networks we're out of. Some rows carry notes, for example *SHARP Health Plan PPO: FirstHealthNetwork, not contracted with Tier 1 or 2, use only Tier 3*.
2. **`Accepted Insurances.docx`** in `V:\EC\FRONT`, `V:\JC\FRONT` and `V:\MP FRONT`. It's from 2022 and **out of date**; for example, it still lists Multiplan and PHCS as not contracted. Use it only as a hint.
3. **Outlook history.** Search appointment subjects for the plan name to see whether we've been seeing patients on that plan and how they were tagged (Cash-Pay, Self Open-Balance, and so on).
4. **The public website's `accepted-insurances.html`**, built from Veradigm payer data. It should agree with (1). If it doesn't, fix the site.

## Reading the card

- Rental networks (First Health, Multiplan/PHCS) appear on cards for **self-funded employer plans**. The claims payer is the administrator (TPA) printed on the card, for example Performance Health. Check eligibility and benefits with that TPA, not with the network.
- A pharmacy logo on the card (STAR-Rx and similar) has nothing to do with our office.
- Record the copay, deductible and out-of-pocket amounts the card shows, so the front desk collects the right amount.

## Worked example (Sept 2026)

A **First Health Network PPO** card administered by a TPA:
- **In network.** First Health Network is on the *APC CONTRACTED PLANS* tab, and we see First Health patients regularly.
- **Collect** the specialist copay shown on the card, after the deductible.
- **Exception:** if the card says **Sharp Health Plan PPO** with First Health as the out-of-area network, only Tier 3 applies (see the Not Contracted tab).
